# 10. Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Ethics

## Core Question

How are artificial intelligence and data science changing computing, and what ethical issues arise?

## Detailed Explanation

Modern computer science extends beyond traditional programming into data-driven and intelligent systems.

### Artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of creating systems that can perform tasks normally associated with human intelligence, such as:

- recognizing images
- understanding language
- making predictions
- playing games
- controlling robots

Examples include:

- speech recognition
- recommendation systems
- autonomous vehicles
- medical diagnosis support

### Machine learning

Machine learning is a major part of AI. It allows systems to learn patterns from data instead of following only explicit instructions.

Example:

- A spam filter learns to identify spam by studying many examples.

### Data science

Data science is the process of collecting, cleaning, analyzing, and interpreting large amounts of data.

It is used for:

- business decisions
- scientific research
- healthcare insights
- forecasting
- marketing analysis

### Ethical concerns

As computing becomes more powerful, ethical issues become more important.

Questions include:

- Is the data fair and unbiased?
- Who is responsible when AI makes a wrong decision?
- Does automation reduce jobs or create new opportunities?
- How do we protect privacy?
- Can AI be used in harmful ways?

### Why ethics matters

Technology should help society, not cause harm. Computer scientists must consider fairness, transparency, accountability, and respect for people.

### In summary

AI and data science are transforming computing, but they must be designed responsibly. Good systems combine technical skill with ethical awareness.

## Key Takeaways

- AI helps computers learn and reason in limited ways.
- Data science uses data to discover patterns and support decisions.
- Ethics is crucial when building powerful technologies.
