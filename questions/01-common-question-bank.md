# Common Questions and Answers

## 1. What is computer science?

Computer science is the study of computation, information, and problem solving. It includes understanding how computers work, how data is represented, how software is built, and how systems communicate.

## 2. Why is binary important?

Binary is important because computers use electrical states that are naturally on/off. Representing everything in binary makes it possible for computers to store and process data.

## 3. What is the difference between hardware and software?

Hardware is the physical equipment of a computer, such as the CPU, memory, and storage. Software is the set of instructions and programs that tell the hardware what to do.

## 4. What is an algorithm?

An algorithm is a step-by-step procedure used to solve a problem. A good algorithm is correct, clear, and efficient.

## 5. What is programming?

Programming is the act of writing instructions in a language that a computer can understand. It involves problem solving, logical thinking, and testing.

## 6. Why do we need data structures?

Data structures help organize information so it can be accessed, processed, and modified efficiently. They are essential for writing effective programs.

## 7. What does an operating system do?

An operating system manages hardware resources, runs programs, organizes files, and provides the interface users interact with.

## 8. What is the internet?

The internet is a global network of connected devices and computers that can communicate and share information.

## 9. Why is software engineering important?

Software engineering ensures that software is built in a structured, reliable, and maintainable way. It reduces mistakes and helps systems scale.

## 10. What is AI and why does it matter?

AI creates systems that can perform tasks that mimic human intelligence. It matters because it affects automation, decision making, healthcare, education, and many other areas.

## 11. How do computers store data?

Computers store data as binary values, usually in bits and bytes. Text, images, audio, and video are all represented using binary patterns.

## 12. What is the role of ethics in computer science?

Ethics helps ensure technology is useful, fair, safe, and respectful of human rights. It is important because computing decisions can affect large numbers of people.

## Quick Revision Tips

- Study one concept at a time.
- Practice examples and pseudocode.
- Relate concepts to real-world software and systems.
- Review definitions often.
- Test yourself with short questions.
